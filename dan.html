Dan (The server Hoster)

Image of Dan's name in the steam server browser (Image taken by Grub PL)

Dan is known for hosting the mysterious SANDDOLLAR server, They have interacted with the players and investigators of the arg on mgen_output And mgen_output_null. Dan Usually gives out hints to solving the puzzle for SANDDOLLAR. Including the time where he referenced the d1_eli_01 version of mgen_output.

He does not appear in the server regularly like Hero Does. The investigation team has only seen him once standing in the center of the map which blocked people from walking around and exploring output_null.
